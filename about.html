Who we are

A non-profit for youth, opportunity and harmony

The Seekers is a Pakistan-based, non-profit, non-sectarian, non-governmental organization founded on 4 July 2015.

We work for sustainable social, economic and environmental development — empowering young people, fighting poverty, and building understanding between every segment of society. People of every religion, caste, colour and region are welcome with us.

Primary objectives

What we set out to do

Empower youth

Career counselling, mentorship and skills development that help young people make informed decisions about their future.

Eradicate poverty

Pathways to education, scholarships, job placements and income generation for families who need them most.

Encourage welfare & harmony

Welfare initiatives and open dialogue that build understanding, unity and pluralism across society.

Our vision

Empowered youth, equal opportunities and a harmonious society

Our mission

To empower individuals through career counselling, education and skills development; eradicate poverty by creating pathways for scholarships, employment and income generation; and foster social welfare and dialogue that promote unity, compassion and sustainable growth.
