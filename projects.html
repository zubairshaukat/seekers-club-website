Project 1 · Environment

Tree Plantation Drive

Our land needs your help. We plant and nurture trees in schools, neighbourhoods and public spaces — and raise awareness of why they matter.

Trees purify the air, support biodiversity and help fight climate change. By involving volunteers — especially young people — we want tree plantation to become a way of life, not a one-day event.

  • Plant and care for trees in communities, schools and public spaces
  • Raise awareness of the role trees play in fighting climate change
  • Engage youth volunteers in caring for the environment
  • Work with local bodies and residents on long-term care of saplings

Project 2 · Summer relief

Bringing hope and hydration

In the scorching summer heat, cool drinking water is a necessity, not a comfort.

Through our Water Cooler Distribution Drive we give water coolers to poor and needy families, so they have cool drinking water through the hottest months. It is a simple act of care that brings immediate relief — and reminds families that they are not forgotten.
